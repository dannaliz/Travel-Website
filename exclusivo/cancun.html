Fun facts about Cancún

Things to share on your trip.

🏗️ A young city

Cancún was officially founded in 1970 as a planned tourism project; before that it was an island with only a few dozen residents.

🐍 The descent of Kukulkán

On the spring and autumn equinoxes, sunlight forms the shadow of a serpent sliding down the pyramid at Chichén Itzá.

🌊 A museum under the sea

MUSA has more than 500 submerged sculptures that work as an artificial reef for corals and fish.

💧 Underground rivers

The Yucatán Peninsula has no surface rivers: water flows underground and emerges in thousands of cenotes.

🐢 Turtle beaches

From May to October, sea turtles nest on the beaches; hotels dim their lights so hatchlings don't get disoriented.

⏰ Its own time zone

Quintana Roo uses UTC−5 all year, one hour ahead of Mexico City.
