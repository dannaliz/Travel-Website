Fun facts about Paris

Things to share on your trip.

🗼 A temporary tower

The Eiffel Tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair and was meant to be dismantled after 20 years; it was saved by its usefulness as a radio antenna.

📏 It grows in summer

In the heat, the iron expands and the tower can "grow" about 15 centimeters.

🖼️ Impossible to see it all

The Louvre displays around 35,000 works; looking at each for 30 seconds would take you more than 12 days non-stop.

💀 A city underground

The Catacombs hold the remains of about six million people in old limestone quarries.

🗽 Lady Liberty's "sister"

On the Île aux Cygnes, in the Seine, stands a smaller replica of the statue France gave the United States.

🥖 The baguette is heritage

In 2022 UNESCO added the craft of baguette-making to its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage.
