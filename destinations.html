Cancún, Mexico

  • 📅 7 days / 6 nights
  • ✈️ Flight + all-inclusive hotel
  • 🌊 Isla Mujeres, Chichén Itzá & Tulum

White-sand beaches and turquoise water in the Mexican Caribbean, combined with visits to Isla Mujeres, the Mayan city of Chichén Itzá and the seaside ruins of Tulum.

Day-by-day itinerary
  1. Arrival. Transfer to the hotel, beach afternoon and welcome dinner.
  2. Rest day. Free day to enjoy the all-inclusive resort.
  3. Isla Mujeres. Catamaran, snorkeling and an afternoon at Playa Norte.
  4. Chichén Itzá. Archaeological site, Ik Kil cenote and the town of Valladolid.
  5. Tulum & Playa del Carmen. Ruins by the sea and a walk along Fifth Avenue.
  6. Free day. Optional activities and farewell dinner.
  7. Return. Breakfast and transfer to the airport.
What's included
  • ✔ Round-trip economy flight
  • ✔ 6 nights all-inclusive
  • ✔ Airport transfers
  • ✔ Guided tours to Isla Mujeres, Chichén Itzá and Tulum
  • ✔ Travel insurance
  • ✘ Environmental tax (Visitax)
  • ✘ Optional activities
  • ✘ Tips and personal expenses

Paris, France

  • 📅 10 days / 9 nights
  • ✈️ Flight + hotel + tours
  • 🎨 Louvre, Versailles & Seine cruise

The City of Light: art, architecture, food and fashion. Walk through Montmartre and Le Marais, enjoy a café by the Seine and visit the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre and the Palace of Versailles.

Day-by-day itinerary
  1. Arrival in Paris. Charles de Gaulle airport, transfer to the hotel and free time.
  2. Eiffel Tower & Seine cruise. Second floor of the tower, Champ de Mars and a sunset cruise.
  3. The Louvre. Guided visit to see the Mona Lisa and the Venus de Milo; afternoon in the Tuileries.
  4. Île de la Cité & Latin Quarter. Notre-Dame, Sainte-Chapelle and historic cafés.
  5. Palace of Versailles. Full-day trip: Hall of Mirrors, royal apartments and gardens.
  6. Montmartre. Sacré-Cœur and Place du Tertre; optional cabaret night.
  7. Champs-Élysées & Arc de Triomphe. Walk, climb the Arc and shopping.
  8. Musée d'Orsay & Le Marais. The world's largest Impressionist collection.
  9. Free day. Optional Disneyland Paris or Loire Valley; farewell dinner.
  10. Return. Breakfast, check-out and airport transfer.
What's included
  • ✔ Round-trip economy flight
  • ✔ 9 nights with breakfast
  • ✔ Airport transfers
  • ✔ Tickets and guided visits: Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Versailles, Orsay
  • ✔ Seine cruise
  • ✔ Travel insurance valid in Europe
  • ✘ Meals not specified
  • ✘ City tourist tax (paid at the hotel)
  • ✘ Optional activities
